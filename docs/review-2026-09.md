# smallcc review, 2026-09-26

Scope: the whole compiler (front end, middle end, back end, driver, tests,
docs), read with three questions: where are the smells and latent bugs, what
can be clarified or simplified without losing generated-code performance, and
what would make a second ISA (CPU5) cheap to add. Four parallel read-throughs
produced the raw findings; every wrong-code claim below was then reproduced
against the checked-in `smallcc` and `sim_c` (section 1), and the performance
claims in section 3 were reproduced the same way. Line references come from
the read-through and were spot-checked, not individually re-verified.

Baseline for "no performance loss": CoreMark 403,373 cycles with the documented
CRCs; ray tracer 65,771,342 instructions per frame.

The one-paragraph verdict: the architecture is right and worth keeping (one
representation per regime, every register constraint an IR value, target-exact
float folding, the measure/commit emission fixpoint, the three-way oracle).
The problems are of two kinds. First, a set of silent wrong-code cases in the
front end and in always-on middle-end passes that the corpus and fuzzer have
not been shaped to catch. Second, target knowledge that is spread across
roughly 60 sites in eleven files with no single source, which is what makes
CPU5 look expensive today. Both are fixable without touching the design.

## 2. Smells and simplifications with no performance cost

Ordered by how much clarity they buy. None of these change generated code.

### 2.1 Front end

- **Five constant-expression evaluators, none complete.** `types.c:301`
  (array sizes), `parser.c:1396` (case labels), `parser.c:1195` (enum
  values), the preprocessor's own (`preprocess.c:434-638`, legitimately
  separate since it works on text), and the cast/negate peelers copied into
  `lower.c` and `braun.c:99-110`. One `const_eval(Node*)` returning
  int / float / symbol+offset / string, used by all of them, fixes 1, 2, 3,
  11 and 12 above at once.
- **Global initialisers laid out by init-list shape instead of by type.**
  `lower.c` recurses one level. Recursing on the `Type` (struct fields,
  array elements) with `const_eval` for leaves is shorter than what is there
  and cannot silently zero-fill.
- **Frame layout is computed in the front end and half ignored.**
  `Symbol_table.size/local_offset/param_offset`, `finalize_local_offsets`,
  `shift_param_offsets_for_struct_ret` (`types.c:1152-1232`) exist, but
  `braun.c:444,1381,2621` keeps its own parameter-home tables and uses the
  front-end param offsets only as a sort key. The front end should record
  declaration order and `Type`; a target-owned frame pass assigns slots.
  This is also where `FRAME_OVERHEAD`, `bp+4` and 4-byte slots leave the
  front end.
- **Global mutable state with hand-choreographed resets.** Three context
  structs plus stray statics (`types.c:8`, `preprocess.c:76-98`,
  `lower.c:21`, `parser.c:1859`); `reset_preprocessor` wipes `-D` macros so
  the driver re-injects them per TU (`smallcc.c:639-660`); `make_basic_types`
  re-appends `va_list` every TU without dedupe (`types.c:602`). One
  `fe_begin_tu()` and a predefined-macro watermark.
- **Duplicated parser mechanics.** The `last_line/last_col/current = next`
  triple appears seven times beside `expect()`; "starts a declaration" is
  spelled four ways; `declaration()`, `param_declaration()` and `type_name()`
  repeat the specifier prologue; `type_name`'s typedef fast path
  (`parser.c:671-684`) rejects `(T **)p` although the general path handles
  it. `advance()`, `starts_declaration()`, `parse_specifiers()`.
- **Literal lexing split across tokeniser and parser.** Suffixes are consumed
  and discarded (`tokeniser.c:446`), then re-derived from the token text
  (`parser.c:275`); `strtol` is host-width; `(int)val[0]` sign-extends by host
  `char` signedness (`parser.c:337`) while case labels use `unsigned char`.
  The token should carry `{uint64 value, suffix flags}` and the parser type
  it from a target table.
- **Error handling.** `error()` has no location and is not `noreturn`, hence
  dead `return 0` after calls; preprocessor errors carry no file:line;
  `type_name`'s `while (!= RPAREN) declarator()` spins on bad input
  (`parser.c:696`). Every "unsupported" branch that currently returns 0 or
  `t_int` should be an error.
- **Vestiges.** `Type.qual` never written; `token_ctx.user_input` never read;
  `ParserContext.current_function` read only by an unreachable fallback;
  `resolve_symbols` re-looks-up identifiers that `unary_expr` already
  resolved; `include/stdlib.h` and `include/stddef.h` define `NULL`
  differently and the winner depends on include order. The Sexp layer
  (`sx.c`, `lower.c`) has one producer and two tag-string consumers
  (`emit.c:2711`, `irsim.c:737`); a typed `GlobalInit` array replaces it.

### 2.2 Middle end

- **Six copies of "look through copies and find the constant"**, each with
  its own hop limit and look-through set: `braun.c:927` (4 hops),
  `opt.c:2494` (8, with TRUNC), `opt.c:1380` (6), `opt.c:679`, `opt.c:1001`,
  `ssa.h:207`, and legalize Pass E (`legalize.c:478`, which once hung on an
  unbounded walk). The same expression is a constant to one pass and not to
  another. `val_strip_copies()` and `val_const()` in `ssa.h`, delete the
  rest. Slightly positive for code: sites that stop at 4 hops will fold.
- **Instruction-kind predicates re-enumerated per pass, inconsistently.**
  Purity in three places (`opt.c:440`, `braun.c:800`, `opt.c:3067`);
  memory-clobber in four (`opt.c:1268` omits `IK_PUTCHAR`); terminators in
  three; comparison detection by enum range in `braun.c:1740`. One table
  next to `InstKind`.
- **`use_count` is owned by everyone.** Manual `use_count++` at
  `opt.c:1231,1431`, direct `ops` array writes at `opt.c:1491` bypassing
  `inst_add_op`, placeholder-then-decrement at `opt.c:2821,3319`, five
  copies of the unlink loop, three of "append phi after phis". State the
  invariant once: passes never touch `use_count`, `recount_uses` owns it, and
  run one unconditional recount after `split_critical_edges` (this is also
  the fix for item 16).
- **"In the loop" means "dominated by the header"** at thirteen sites
  (`opt.c:228,1577,1608,1634,1689,1751,1798,1820,1841,1889,2618,2749,2765`),
  which includes the exit block and everything after the loop. Post-loop pure
  code gets hoisted above the loop and holds a register through it; the LICM
  budgets (`nlive`, `nloop_defs`, `body_insts`) are computed over inflated
  sets and were tuned against those inflated numbers. `loop_body_marks`
  (`opt.c:193`) already computes the real body and is used by only two
  passes. Compute it once in `find_loops`, use it everywhere, then retune.
  Must land together with the MMIO guard for item 13, because today the
  spin loops survive only because the post-loop stores make the loop look
  non-store-free.
- **Three private register-pressure estimators** (`opt.c:1555`, `1745`,
  `2723`) with different caps and budgets written as literals `8`, `6`,
  `8 - 4 - nlive`. One `LoopPressure` filled by `find_loops`, budgets as
  `K - reserve - nlive`. Prerequisite for K=16.
- **`braun.c` is five modules.** String-literal table and data rendering
  (`18-283`), inline registry and expansion (`286-465, 1417-1469`), local
  value numbering, the algebraic folder (`800-1012`), jump-table policy, and
  the incoming-argument ABI layout (`2631-2757`), with `cg_expr` at 544
  lines. `data.c`, `inline.c`, `fold.c` (which emit P7 should then call
  instead of carrying its own copy with the same SHR bug), and `cg_expr`
  split by node kind.
- **Vestigial IR.** `IK_ZEXT` is never produced and `IK_NOT` never reaches
  emission, yet both have handlers in opt, legalize, irsim and emit.
  `CallDesc.hidden_sret` is set and never read. `Inst.imm` doubles as MEMCPY
  size and TRUNC mask while legalize D and irsim recompute the width from
  vtypes. Compare results are typed `VT_I16` and `vt_of(NULL)` is `VT_I16`
  with `: 2` defaults at fourteen sites, all LP32 leftovers; the `VT_I16`
  booleans create type-coercing copies that `opt_copy_prop` refuses to
  propagate (`opt.c:415`), so fixing this is a small code win.
- **`irsim.c` duplicates what it should import**: `vt_size` (different
  default from `vtype_size`), stubs for `strlen/strcmp/...` that are always
  compiled from `lib/`, and the shift-signedness-by-vtype rule that also
  lives in `emit.c:310`. Shift signedness is encoded twice (`IK_SHR` vs
  `IK_USHR` and the vtype); make `IK_SHR` unconditionally arithmetic.
- **`opt_jump_thread` does not do what its comment says** (`opt.c:1941` vs
  `2019-2059`): copies in the thin block are bypassed, not pulled up, and
  `value_defined_in` keys on `dst->def` of a multi-def OOS variable so
  threading fires for one arbitrary predecessor. Not reproduced as wrong
  code; latent.

### 2.3 Back end

- **`emit_inst` is a 628-line function whose binary-op case is a 230-line
  if-chain with two notions of "constant"** (`c0/c1` for VAL_CONST driving
  P2, P3, P4, P18, P19; `k0/k1` including IK_CONST driving P7, P8, P14, P15;
  `resolve_const` for P11, P13). The table-driven selector in section 4
  turns this into fold, bitex, known-bits, `select_form`, three-register
  fallback, with identical output by construction.
- **`mark_dead_consts` (`emit.c:1492-1553`) must mirror `emit_inst`'s
  decisions by hand**, a contract stated in a comment at `emit.c:407-411`.
  Same selector, same answer, one call.
- **Sizing by re-parsing the emitted text with a hand-kept mnemonic table.**
  `parse_block_offsets` (`emit.c:1730-1792`) tokenises the memstream and
  `cpu4_mnem_bytes` (`emit.c:1701-1722`) guesses lengths. It lacks `neg`,
  `frecip`, `frsqrt`, `zxwor`, `sxwor`, `imms` and `zero0..7`, all of which
  are emitted, and counts `word`/`align` directives as instructions.
  Over-estimates are conservative today, but every ISA change must be
  mirrored here by hand and the compiler still does not consume `isa.py`.
  Route every instruction through one `emit_op()` that appends text and adds
  the generated byte count; block sizes fall out and both functions go.
- **Emission side tables index two different spaces**: `fuse[]`,
  `block_size[]`, `no_rotate[]`, `g_dbnz_latch[]` by layout position;
  `block_start[]` by `Block.id`; `g_bitex_*` by value id. `detect_dbnz`
  mixes them in one expression (`emit.c:2232`). One `EmitBlockInfo` per
  layout slot.
- **Emit-time register borrowing is not fully accounted for now that call
  clobbers are per-callee.** Prologue `lea r0` (`emit.c:2316`) and epilogue
  `lea r3` (`emit.c:2427`) are not recorded in `g_scratch_borrows` (contrast
  `emit.c:138,153,884`); `pick_scratch` for constant stack args avoids only
  argument registers; the `pushr/popr` fallback (`emit.c:353`) exists
  because the IR never promised a free register. The fuzzer has hit this
  class twice (`emit.c:109`, `alloc.h:27`). Finish the "every scratch is an
  IR value" discipline: Pass B materialises constant stack args as
  `IK_CONST`; out-of-range prologue saves use an IR address value.
- **Dead or defensive code.** VAL_CONST base paths in load/store emission
  cannot fire (braun materialises them); `is_copy` always 1 (`alloc.c:314`);
  `zero_side` unused; `ig_free` a no-op; `is_pure_kind` duplicated between
  legalize and alloc; the unlink idiom inlined three times beside
  `unlink_inst`; `getenv` inside per-instruction loops
  (`legalize.c:171,215,297,359`, `alloc.c:79`); `preg()` returns r0 for an
  unallocated value (`emit.c:162`), which forced the workaround at
  `alloc.c:1177`. Make it an assertion.
- **alloc.c is about 90% target-neutral**, which is the good news. The
  residue is enumerable: the in-place-hint kind list (`alloc.c:575-598`), the
  switch clobber mask (`434`), the return register (`1127`), the F2 spill
  range (`825-829`), the `0x0F` caller-saved literals, and the `uint8_t`
  masks. `is_rematerializable` treats every `IK_CONST` as free
  (`alloc.c:900`) while `emit_imm` costs 1 to 6 bytes; a `const_cost` hook
  lets remat and spill cost agree.

### 2.4 Infrastructure and docs

- **`-arch cpu4` is accepted and switches nothing** (`smallcc.c:437-443`).
  What actually varies by target is spread over `smallcc.h:50-52`
  (`INT_SIZE`, `PTR_SIZE`, `FRAME_OVERHEAD`), `alloc.h:19-20`,
  `legalize.c:294`, `braun.c:16` and `irsim.c:29` (the FPU model include),
  the crt0 filename (`smallcc.c:599`), the built-in preamble
  (`smallcc.c:611`) and all of emit.c.
- **Makefile has no header dependencies** beyond the two FPU headers; editing
  `ssa.h` or `opt.h` does not rebuild. `fuzz` is missing from `.PHONY` and
  `help`; `test_p`/`test_v` skip `isa-check`.
- **conftest.py**: `CPU4_MAXSTEPS` is defined and never passed; the corpus
  `TIMEOUT:` key is silently ignored (two files use it); `CFLAGS` and
  `SIM_ARGS` are honoured but undocumented; the `arch` field doubles as an
  irsim mode name; a comment references a `-Opass=fmadd` that no longer
  exists.
- **Corpus hygiene**: `tests/cases/coremark/state_simple` is a checked-in
  Mach-O binary; `fuzz_failures/` is not gitignored; `lib/crt0_cpu4_sim.s`
  is referenced by nothing.
- **Pass IDs and bit tables are hand-duplicated in four places** (`opt.h`,
  the `pass_tbl` in `smallcc.c:506`, `optimization-passes.md`, the measure
  script); `opt.h` labels LICM as R2K, the same ID as known-bits. An X-macro
  list generates the bits, the CLI table and an `-Olist`.
- **The verifier does not check what legalize establishes**: nothing asserts
  post-legalize that `IK_NOT`, float `IK_NEG` and narrow ZEXT/TRUNC are gone
  or that param and call-arg copies carry the expected `phys_reg`. Cheap
  checks under a new `VERIFY_POST_LEGALIZE` phase. `verify.h` also
  overstates what the use_count check does (undercount only).
- **Documentation drift**: 34 concrete disagreements between the docs and
  the code, listed in Appendix A. The worst: three documents say peepholes
  end at P18 (code has P21); the pipeline doc's Pass C description (integer
  NEG lowered) is the opposite of the code; the frame diagram in
  `compiler-pipeline.md` contradicts both `cpu4.md` and `emit.c`;
  `docs/abi.md` says named variadic parameters travel in registers, the code
  passes all of them on the stack; the file table in CLAUDE.md lists a
  `lib/crt0.s` that does not exist and omits eight files that do.
- **Corpus gaps** worth filling regardless of the fixes above: no test runs
  at anything but the default mask (no `CFLAGS: -O0` duplicates); zero
  dedicated tests for `unroll`, `licm`, `jump_thread`, `lsr`, `addr_iv`,
  `known_bits`, `bitwise_dist`, `redundant_bool`, `narrow_loads`, Pass F at
  the range boundaries, or `scalar_promote`; `errors/` has one test;
  `emit/` has three tests for 21 peepholes (no P16 sign-leak boundary, no
  P17/P21 out-of-range fallback, no P20 with the counter also used in the
  body); `io/` has one; `multifile/` has one pair; irsim has no step cap so
  a hang is a hang rather than a failure. The fuzzer generates unsigned-only,
  no floats, no structs, no switch, no calls with more than three arguments,
  which is precisely why section 1 was not found earlier.

## 3. Simplifications that also improve generated code

Each of these was reproduced. They are simplifications first; the code
improvement is a side effect of removing an inconsistency.

1. **Immediate-form knowledge is triplicated and already out of sync.**
   Legalize Pass F keeps ADD/SUB constants in [-512, 511] unmaterialised
   (`legalize.c:671-677`, comment "P2/P3/P4 handle |k| <= 511"), but the
   emitter's widest add immediate is `addli` at +-256. Reproduced: in a loop,
   `y += 200` emits one `addli`; `x += 300` emits `immw r0, 300; add` on
   every iteration, and the constant cannot be hoisted because it is a
   VAL_CONST, not an `IK_CONST`. Same family: masks 256..32767 that bitex
   will consume are materialised into registers that `detect_bitex_fusions`
   then kills. One `select_form()` over a table (section 4) called from Pass
   F, the allocator's hints, `mark_dead_consts` and `emit_inst` ends this
   class.
2. **Callee-save detection runs before emit-time dead-constant marking**
   (`emit.c:2591` before `2593-2597`). A LICM-hoisted mask that lives across
   a call and is then folded by P8/P11/P13/P16 still costs an `sl` and `ll`
   pair on every call to the function. Split detection from emission and run
   it after the peepholes.
3. **`IK_MEMCPY` moves halfwords only** (`emit.c:980-988`). Reproduced: a
   12-byte aligned vec3 copy is 12 `llw`/`slw` pairs where 6 word moves would
   do; the index also overflows the F3c range past 1022 bytes (assembler
   error). Carry alignment on the memcpy and use word moves for the aligned
   prefix. Halves struct-copy cost in by-value code.
4. **Sub-word spill slots buy nothing** (`alloc.c:815-819`): 2-byte slots for
   narrow types halve the F2 reach, need a signedness rule on reload, and
   already produced the VT_PTR bug. Spill everything as 4 bytes; delete
   `spill_size` and the size-dependent branches.
5. **The loop-body definition** (2.2) is a code win once fixed and retuned,
   because post-loop values stop occupying hoist slots and registers.
6. **Branch-range estimation omits the branch's own block for backward
   branches and estimates a self-loop at zero bytes** (`emit.c:1930-1938`,
   `2029-2038`), hidden by the 500/450 caps at `1943,2042`. Using the
   measured `block_start`/`block_size` exactly as `detect_dbnz` already does
   (`emit.c:2232`) lets the caps go to the true +-511 and lands more fusions.
7. **`VT_I16` booleans** (2.2) block copy propagation on every compare that
   flows into an `int`.

| Memory map | `0xF000` and `0xFF00` in four crt0/putc files, `irsim.c:35-38,580` | generated from `docs/memory-map.md` |

Two things are structural rather than descriptor fields. The register mask
width (`uint8_t` everywhere) must widen before anything else. And the
emission fixpoint assumes an unlimited-range unfused branch always exists
(`jnz`/`j` absolute, `emit.c:2151,1096`); it only shrinks. A pc-relative-only
CPU5 needs a relaxation step that can grow, which the loop does not have.

### 4.2 Proposed shape

```c
typedef uint32_t regmask_t;

/* Generated from <arch>/isa.py by gen_isa.py, next to isa_table_c.h. */
typedef struct { const char *mnem; uint8_t bytes; ImmKind ik; uint8_t bits, scale; } OpInfo;

/* Hand-written per target, checked at build against isa.py (mnemonic exists,
   [lo,hi] within imm_range). Ordered by ascending bytes = priority. */
typedef struct {
    InstKind kind; uint8_t const_pos;      /* lhs, rhs, either */
    Shape shape;                            /* RRR, RRI, RI_INPLACE, R_INPLACE, RR_DUP */
    int16_t op; int32_t lo, hi; int8_t adjust; uint8_t flags;  /* EXACT_K, NEGATE_K, SIGN_SAFE_REQ ... */
} AluForm;
typedef struct { InstKind kind; uint8_t operand_shape; int16_t op, inv_op; } BranchForm;

typedef struct Target {
    const char *name;
    int nregs; regmask_t allocatable, caller_saved, callee_saved;
    int ret_reg, fp_reg; const int *arg_regs; int n_arg_regs; regmask_t switch_clobbers;
    int ptr_size, stack_slot, frame_align, first_stack_param_off;
    int (*bp_slot_reachable)(int off, int size);
    int (*const_cost)(int32_t k);
    const OpInfo *ops; const AluForm *alu; const BranchForm *br;
    int br_disp_bits, br_disp_unit, has_abs_branch;
    unsigned caps;                          /* HAS_DBNZ | HAS_CBZ | HAS_CMP_IMM | HAS_FMA ... */
    const char *(*regname)(int);
    void (*emit_const)(Emitter*, int rd, int32_t k);
    void (*emit_symaddr)(Emitter*, int rd, const char *sym);
    void (*emit_prologue)(Emitter*, Function*, int frame, regmask_t saves);
    void (*emit_epilogue)(Emitter*, Function*, int frame, regmask_t saves);
    void (*emit_call)(Emitter*, Inst*);
    void (*emit_switch)(Emitter*, Inst*, int jt_id);
    void (*emit_memcpy)(Emitter*, Inst*);
} Target;
```

`select_form(target, kind, const_pos, k, inplace, is_signed, sign_safe)` is
the single query, used by legalize Pass F, the allocator's in-place hints,
`mark_dead_consts` and `emit_inst`. On CPU4 the rows are P2 (inc/dec,
EXACT_K), P3 (addi), P4 (addli, SUB via NEGATE_K), P18 (mulli), P8
(zxb/zxw/zxwor), P14 (andi/andli), P11 (shli/shlli), P13 (shrsi/shrsli/shrli
with SIGN_SAFE_REQ), and all of P19. The branch table absorbs
`fused_branch_mnemonic`, the P21 pairs, cbeq/cbne and jz/jnz.

Stays hand-written because it is pattern or layout logic, not encoding: P7
fold (calling the shared `fold.c`), P15 known-bits, P16 bitex (also the
template for a CPU5 fused multiply-add: `IK_FMUL` feeding `IK_FADD`, single
use, same block), P10 load-extends, P1/P12/P20 layout, the fixpoint loop fed
by `Emitter.bytes` instead of text parsing, and the six hooks.

With that in place CPU5 is: a generated `OpInfo[]` from `cpu5/isa.py`, a
hand-written `AluForm[]` and `BranchForm[]`, `nregs = 16`, `br_disp_unit = 2`,
capability bits, the six hooks, a crt0, and a `Target` selected by `-arch`.
Legalize, alloc, emit logic unchanged. Channel instructions arrive the way
`frecip` did: builtin, IR kind, table row.

### 4.3 Order of work

The order matters because some steps make others cheap and one pair must
land together.

1. Corpus entries for every row of section 1 (rule 5), and `CFLAGS: -O0`
   twins for the loop tests. Fix the front-end items through `const_eval`
   and the per-operator typing rules; fix items 13 to 17 in the middle end.
   The LICM MMIO guard and the loop-body definition land in one commit with
   the budgets retuned against CoreMark and the frame count.
2. Widen register masks to `regmask_t`, replace `IRC_K` and the literal 8s
   and 6s with `K - reserve`. No behaviour change; verifiable by identical
   assembly output on CoreMark and the ray tracer.
3. `fold.c`, `val_const()`, the kind predicate table, `recount_uses`
   ownership. Same identical-output check.
4. `OpInfo[]` generated from `isa.py`, `emit_op()` with byte accounting,
   delete `cpu4_mnem_bytes` and `parse_block_offsets`. Fusion counts may
   rise; output must not regress.
5. The form tables and `select_form()`; collapse the P2 to P19 chain and
   `mark_dead_consts`; Pass F and the allocator hints call it. This is where
   3.1 gets fixed. Identical or better output.
6. `Target` struct with the six hooks; `-arch` selects it; `conftest` loops
   `ARCHES`. CPU4 is the only entry. Identical output.
7. CPU5.

Steps 2 to 6 are each a mechanical refactor with an exact oracle: the
assembled bytes of CoreMark and the ray tracer before and after. That is the
same method used for the encoding-table change and it should be the
acceptance test for every one of them.

## 5. What to keep

- One representation per regime and the two hard boundaries (tree to Braun
  SSA, SSA to virtual registers); `ValType` and `CallDesc` as the only type
  residue past Braun.
- Every register constraint is a pre-coloured IR value; phantom-node
  clobbers with per-callee masks. This is what makes IRC target-neutral, and
  the borrowing finding in 2.3 is about finishing it, not changing it.
- `val_resolve` aliasing with `recount_uses` as the one cleanup, enforced by
  the verifier's undercount check. The fix in 2.2 applies it one step
  earlier.
- `find_loops` and `loop_body_marks` refusing an under-approximated body.
- Target-exact float folding and interpretation through `fpu_model.h`;
  `remove_edge_paired` with its documented failure case.
- The measure/commit emission fixpoint with sticky `no_rotate` and dbnz
  decisions; only its measurement source should change.
- The three-way oracle, fuzz seeds quoted at the fix site, `p7_fold`'s
  "fires independently of operand values" contract, Pass H's explicit
  escape reasoning.
- Front end: the data-driven AST walker (`node_shapes`, `for_each_child`,
  `post_order_walk`), structural type interning with pointer equality across
  TUs, the open-addressing symbol hash, the zeroed arena, and linemarker
  source locations with the caret re-read from the original file.
